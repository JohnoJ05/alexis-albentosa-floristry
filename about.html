About me

Hello, I'm Lexi

Hi, I’m Lexi. A passionate florist striving to create beautiful, contemporary flowers for weddings and events. My career in floristry began running a floristry shop, providing bouquets daily, and running bouquet workshops for like-minded people.

I now specialise in weddings and events, as my passion sparks when working closely with clients, building relationships with people, and creating beautiful, intimate experiences with flowers. Over the past year, I have worked on a number of intimate weddings, and have big dreams to grow my business in the weddings and events industry.

Alongside weddings, I also provide bespoke work for businesses: dressing event spaces, setting tables and providing luxury bouquets, with my most recent customer being Flannels Leeds, dressing their private shopping suite. I also work closely with the independent restaurant The Cut and Craft Leeds, where I host workshops and dress their private dining room.
