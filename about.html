About me

Hello, I'm Alexis

I've always known I had a love for flowers. My journey started when I came home from a long stint of travelling and decided I needed a career that fulfilled my creativity.

[PORTRAIT OF YOU WITH FLOWERS]

I've experimented with all sorts of floristry styles, from classic elegant shaping to wild, whimsical florals. Over time I've naturally found myself drawn to the more natural, whimsy-looking designs. I'm all about movement, colour and giving my customers florals that really take their breath away.

My background

How I got here

Chapter 1

Finding floristry

Back from travelling and looking for something creative, I booked onto Joseph Massie's career course, and it opened my eyes to what floristry could be.

Chapter 2

Owning a floristry shop

I jumped straight into owning a fast-growing, successful floristry shop. After lots of hard work and research, I left the shop to go self-employed in weddings and events, my clear passion.

Chapter 3

Alexis Albentosa Floristry

Over the past three years I completed a specialised 3D floristry course at Leeds Arts University, spent a year travelling and creating, and have spent this last year growing my business and my skill set.
